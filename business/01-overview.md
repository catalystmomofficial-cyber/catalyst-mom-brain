# Overview

## What Catalyst Mom Is

Catalyst Mom is a comprehensive digital maternal wellness platform built to
support women through every stage of motherhood — **Trying to Conceive
(TTC)**, **Pregnancy**, and **Postpartum recovery**. It bridges the gap
between acute clinical postpartum care and mainstream fitness by applying
evidence-based, stage-specific protocols built around real women and real
results.

Catalyst Mom rejects generic fitness advice and treats mothers as active,
recovering powerhouses undergoing profound biological transformation.

- Primary app: https://catalystmomofficial.com
- Assessment funnel: https://catalystmom.online

## Mission

To guide women through every stage of motherhood with evidence-based
protocols that drive tangible health results. We provide the structure,
expertise, and community necessary to transform recovery and performance
during the TTC, pregnancy, and postpartum journeys.

## Core Brand Line

> "Built from love — for moms, with moms."

## The Three Stages

### Stage 1 — Trying to Conceive (TTC)
**Program:** Fertile Ground — 30-Day Conception Prep
**Focus:** Cycle awareness, fertility-friendly movement, hormone-supporting
nutrition, nervous system regulation
**Key features:** Cycle tracker, phase-synced workouts, fertility nutrition
guides, ovulation awareness tools

### Stage 2 — Pregnancy
**Program:** Birth Ready — Trimester-by-Trimester Fitness Guide
**Focus:** Safe prenatal movement, birth preparation, pelvic floor training,
labour positioning
**Key features:** Trimester-specific workouts, birth ball guide, hospital bag
checklist, birth preference template, contraction tracker, baby kick counter
**Trimesters covered:** First (weeks 1-13), Second (weeks 14-26), Third
(weeks 27-40), Labour preparation

### Stage 3 — Postpartum
**Program:** Core Restore — 30-Day Postpartum Recovery
**Focus:** Diastasis recti healing, pelvic floor restoration, core
reconnection, safe progressive strength
**Key features:** DR self-check guide, C-section and vaginal birth
modifications, core-safe exercise protocols, return to exercise checklist

## What Catalyst Mom Is NOT

- Not a medical diagnosis tool
- Not a replacement for obstetric or pelvic floor physiotherapy care
- Not a generic fitness app
- Not built for audiences outside TTC, Pregnancy, and Postpartum stages

## Proof Points

- 2,000+ mamas supported across all three stages
- Protocols aligned with ACOG (American College of Obstetricians and
  Gynecologists) guidelines and NASM (National Academy of Sports Medicine)
  standards — see `07-safety-and-compliance.md`
