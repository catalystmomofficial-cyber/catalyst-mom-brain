## Clinical Framework Highlights

### Diastasis Recti (DR) Recovery
- DR = separation of the rectus abdominis muscles along the linea alba.
- Affects up to **70%** of women in immediate postpartum; ~**32%** still
  affected at 12 months.
- Traditional crunches, sit-ups, and standard planks increase intra-abdominal
  pressure and **worsen** separation.
- Catalyst Mom approach: progressive tension loading across the myofascial
  web using deep core activation, diaphragmatic breathing, and
  stage-appropriate movement.

### Delivery Type Tracks
- **C-Section Core Restore Track:** Releases restrictive scar tissue, bypasses
  unsafe loading zones, safely closes deep tissue gaps without compounding
  midline stress.
- **Vaginal Core and Floor Track:** Re-coordinates deep transversus abdominis
  recruitment with pelvic floor relaxation/activation to reverse postpartum
  pooch.

### Sample Protocol — Week 2 ("The Secure Phase")
- Daily movements: 360° Diaphragmatic Breathing, Supine Vacuums/Co-contractions,
  Bent Knee Fall-Outs, Glute Bridges with Core Zip, Quadruped Belly Lifts
- Session duration: 12-15 minutes
- Principle: slow, tension-loaded control over momentum to target deep
  myofascial layers safely
