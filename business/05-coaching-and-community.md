# Coaching, Community, Affiliate & Gamification

## Human Coaching
- Bi-weekly 1-on-1 check-ins with a real wellness expert
- First check-in within the first 2 weeks of joining
- Stage-specific expert titles:
  - Postpartum: **Postnatal Wellness Expert**
  - Pregnancy: **Prenatal Specialist**
  - TTC: **Fertility Wellness Expert**
- 4 qualified experts currently on team
- 14-day consistency milestone triggers a **1:1 Progression Sync** booking

## AI Coaching — "Catalyst"
- 24/7 instant wellness performance guidance
- Personalised using `assessment_data` from the user's Supabase profile
- See `02-brand-voice.md` for tone/persona rules

## Community
2,000+ mamas supported across all three stages. Structured around four main
groups:
- TTC Community
- Pregnancy Community
- Postpartum Community
- Mom Life General

## Live Events
Catalyst Mom hosts live expert events for members across all three stages.
Events are not always publicly listed — announced inside the app and
community first.
- Topics: diastasis recti recovery, birth preparation, fertility wellness,
  pelvic floor health, postpartum mental wellness, nutrition by stage
- Featured specialists: OBGYNs, midwives, pelvic floor physiotherapists,
  prenatal/postnatal fitness specialists
- Members register inside the app and earn points for attendance
- Used as a pitch tool for expert partnerships
- Disclosure rules: see `02-brand-voice.md`

## Gamification & Retention
Points and rewards system drives daily engagement and habit formation.

**How users earn points:**
- Completing daily workouts
- Logging mood, sleep, self-care, and hydration
- Daily wellness check-ins
- Completing profile milestones
- Reaching weekly workout targets
- First check-in with wellness coach

**Badges:**
- Community Member
- Rising Star
- Points Collector
- Core Foundation Complete (postpartum milestone)
- Catalyst Graduate (program completion)

The 14-day consistency milestone triggers a 1:1 Progression Sync — a live
session with the user's stage-specific wellness expert to review data,
celebrate consistency, and calibrate their health blueprint going forward.
