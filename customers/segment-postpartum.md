# Segment — Postpartum

## Likely Primary Goals
- Heal diastasis recti / "get my core back"
- Return to exercise safely after C-section or vaginal birth
- Rebuild strength without making things worse

## Likely Biggest Obstacles
- Don't know if they have DR or how bad it is (Self-Check Guide addresses
  this directly)
- Past attempts at "bouncing back" with generic fitness made things worse
  (crunches/planks worsening separation)
- Time/energy constraints of new motherhood (sleep deprivation, feeding
  schedules)
- Emotional/mental load of the fourth trimester

## Birth Experience Split
The assessment captures `birth_experience` (C-section vs. vaginal), which
determines the track:
- **C-Section Core Restore Track** — scar tissue release, avoids unsafe
  loading zones
- **Vaginal Core and Floor Track** — transversus abdominis + pelvic floor
  recoordination

## What Resonates
- The "up to 70% of women have DR, 32% still at 12 months" stat — validates
  that this is common, not a personal failure
- "Crunches and planks can make DR worse" — directly counters what most new
  moms have been told to do
- Daily Planner — addresses the chaos of new-baby life directly
- Short session lengths (12-15 min) — fits realistic postpartum schedules
