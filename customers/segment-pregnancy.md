# Segment — Pregnancy

## Likely Primary Goals
- Stay safely active throughout pregnancy
- Prepare physically and mentally for birth
- Manage pregnancy-specific discomfort (back pain, sciatica)

## Likely Biggest Obstacles
- Uncertainty about what's "safe" at each trimester
- Anxiety about birth / lack of a birth plan
- Physical discomfort limiting movement
- Information overload from generic pregnancy content

## What Resonates
- Trimester-specific guidance (removes the "is this safe for me right now"
  anxiety)
- Tangible birth-prep deliverables: Hospital Bag Checklist, Birth Preference
  Template, Birth Ball Guide
- Pelvic floor training tied directly to labour prep, not just "general
  wellness"
- Pregnancy anxiety resources — normalizes a common but under-addressed
  experience
