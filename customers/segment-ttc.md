# Segment — Trying to Conceive (TTC)

## Likely Primary Goals
- Get pregnant — understand and optimize fertile window
- Build a body/lifestyle that supports conception
- Reduce stress that may be affecting fertility

## Likely Biggest Obstacles
- Irregular or poorly understood cycle
- Stress/anxiety around trying to conceive
- Not knowing what movement/nutrition is "safe" or helpful while TTC
- Partner not engaged in the process

## What Resonates
- Cycle-phase-synced approach (not generic "fertility diet" content)
- Nervous system regulation — stress is a known fertility factor
- Practical tools: cycle tracker, ovulation awareness guide
- Partner Support Guide — addresses the "doing this alone" pain point

## Coaching Touchpoint
First check-in with **Fertility Wellness Expert** within 2 weeks — important
because TTC can be an emotionally isolating experience; early human contact
matters for retention.

## Messaging Notes
- Avoid any language implying guaranteed conception outcomes — see

  problems."
