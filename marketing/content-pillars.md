# Content Pillars by Stage

Each pillar maps to resources in `../business/05-coaching-and-community.md`
and the program details in `../products/`. Use these as content buckets when
planning posts, pins, or campaigns. Always route content toward the relevant
stage's assessment (catalystmom.online).

## TTC (Fertile Ground)
1. **Cycle awareness** — Cycle Tracking Starter Guide, the four cycle phases
2. **Fertility nutrition** — hormone-supporting foods
3. **Stress & fertility** — cortisol's impact on conception
4. **Ovulation awareness** — reading the body's natural signals
5. **Partner support** — how partners can help during TTC
6. **Pre-conception readiness** — body prep checklist

## Pregnancy (Birth Ready)
1. **Trimester-specific movement** — safe exercise by week/trimester
2. **Birth preparation** — birth ball positioning, hospital bag, birth
   preference template
3. **Pelvic floor training** — prenatal prep
4. **Pregnancy nutrition** — key nutrients by trimester
5. **Pregnancy anxiety** — evidence-based mental wellness tools
6. **Aches & pains** — sciatica/back pain relief

## Postpartum (Core Restore)
1. **Diastasis recti education** — the "70% of women" stat is a strong hook;
   self-check guide
2. **C-section vs. vaginal recovery** — delivery-specific tracks
3. **Why crunches/planks are bad postpartum** — strong differentiator vs.
   generic fitness content
4. **Daily structure for new moms** — daily planner, feeding/recovery
   windows
5. **Sleep strategies**
6. **Postpartum nutrition & hydration**
7. **Mindfulness** — 5-minute practices
8. **Return to exercise** — checklist-driven content

## Cross-Stage / Brand Pillars
- **Real results from 2,000+ mamas** — social proof, testimonials
- **ACOG/NASM-aligned** — credibility/authority content
- **"Built from love — for moms, with moms"** — founder story, community
  spotlight
- **Affiliate / income opportunity** — only for income-seeking audiences, see
  disclosure rules in `../business/02-brand-voice.md`

## Notes for Agents Drafting Content
- Match tone from `../business/02-brand-voice.md` exactly.
- Don't make medical claims beyond what's in
  `../business/07-safety-and-compliance.md`.
- Every piece of content should have a clear next step: take the
  stage-specific assessment at catalystmom.online.
