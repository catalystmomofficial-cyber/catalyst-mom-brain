## App Subscription — $29/month or $249/year
The full platform — all three stage programs, dashboard, workouts, recipes,
wellness tracking, community, progress tracking, affiliate dashboard, AI
coach ("Catalyst"), and bi-weekly human coaching.

- **Cancellation:** Subscribers retain video access after cancellation. Full
  access requires an active subscription.
- **Guarantee:** 30-day money-back guarantee on all new subscriptions.
- **Annual savings:** $249/year vs. $348/year if paying monthly (~28% savings)
  — useful for annual-upgrade messaging.

## App Sections (what subscribers get access to)

- Wellness tracking (mood, sleep, self-care, hydration)
- Community
