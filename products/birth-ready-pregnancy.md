# Birth Ready — Trimester-by-Trimester Fitness Guide

**Stage:** Pregnancy

## Who it's for
Pregnant women who want safe, trimester-appropriate movement and real birth
preparation — not a one-size-fits-all "prenatal yoga" program.

## What's included
- Trimester-specific workouts:
  - First trimester (weeks 1-13)
  - Second trimester (weeks 14-26)
  - Third trimester (weeks 27-40)
  - Labour preparation
- Birth Ball Guide (positioning techniques for labour)
- Hospital Bag Checklist
- Birth Preference Template (how to communicate your birth plan)
- Pelvic Floor Training Guide (prenatal floor prep)
- Contraction tracker
- Baby kick counter
- Nutrition During Pregnancy (key nutrients by trimester)
- Managing Pregnancy Anxiety guide
- Sciatica and Back Pain Relief Guide

## Focus Areas
- Safe prenatal movement
- Birth preparation
- Pelvic floor training
- Labour positioning

## Coaching
Stage-specific expert: **Prenatal Specialist** (bi-weekly 1-on-1 check-ins,
first check-in within 2 weeks of joining). See
`../business/05-coaching-and-community.md`.

## Community
Pregnancy Community group — women at the same stage, same challenges.

## How to talk about this program
- Emphasize "trimester-appropriate" — movement and tools change as the
  pregnancy progresses, not a static plan.
- Birth preparation tools (ball guide, preference template, hospital bag
  checklist) are tangible, practical deliverables — good for content hooks.
- Always defer to OB/healthcare provider clearance language — see
  `../business/07-safety-and-compliance.md`.
