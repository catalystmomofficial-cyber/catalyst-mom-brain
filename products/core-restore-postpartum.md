# Core Restore — 30-Day Postpartum Recovery

**Stage:** Postpartum

## Who it's for
New mothers recovering from birth (C-section or vaginal) who need a safe,
progressive path back to core and pelvic floor strength — especially those
dealing with diastasis recti (DR).

## Pricing
- **$35 one-time purchase** as a standalone 30-day program
- Also included in the full app subscription ($29/mo or $249/yr)

## What's included
- Diastasis Recti (DR) Self-Check Guide
- C-section and vaginal birth modification tracks
- Core-safe exercise protocols (progressive tension loading, not crunches)
- Return to Exercise Checklist (C-section and vaginal versions)
- Daily Planner for new moms (feeding schedules, recovery windows, self-care)
- Sleep Strategies for New Moms
- Nutrition for Postpartum Recovery
- Hydration and Energy Guide
- 5-Minute Mindfulness Practices

## Focus Areas
- Diastasis recti healing
- Pelvic floor restoration
- Core reconnection
- Safe progressive strength

Postpartum Community group — women at the same stage, same challenges.

## How to talk about this program
- This is the program most likely to be sold as a standalone $35 entry point
  — good for top-of-funnel offers.
- Lead with the DR stat (up to 70% affected) — it's relatable and
  underdiscussed.
- Explicitly contrast with generic fitness advice: "crunches and planks can
  make this worse" is a strong, differentiating message.
- Always pair safety claims with `../business/07-safety-and-compliance.md`
  guidance (medical clearance required).
