# Fertile Ground — 30-Day Conception Prep

**Stage:** Trying to Conceive (TTC)

## Who it's for
Women actively trying to conceive who want to prepare their body, cycle
awareness, and nervous system for conception — not generic "fertility
fitness" content.

## What's included
- Cycle tracker
- Phase-synced workouts (movement matched to menstrual cycle phase)
- Fertility-friendly nutrition guides (hormone-supporting foods)
- Ovulation awareness tools
- Stress and fertility education (cortisol's impact on conception)
- Partner Support Guide
- Pre-Conception Health Checklist

## Focus Areas
- Cycle awareness
- Fertility-friendly movement
- Hormone-supporting nutrition
- Nervous system regulation

## Coaching
Stage-specific expert: **Fertility Wellness Expert** (bi-weekly 1-on-1
check-ins, first check-in within 2 weeks of joining). See
`../business/05-coaching-and-community.md`.

## Community
TTC Community group — women at the same stage, same challenges.

## How to talk about this program
- Lead with cycle awareness and working *with* the body's natural rhythm, not
  against it.
- Avoid clinical/diagnostic language (e.g., don't imply this treats
  infertility — see `../business/07-safety-and-compliance.md`).
- Tie into the assessment funnel: a TTC visitor takes the TTC assessment at
  catalystmom.online and gets a personalised Maternal Wellness Score before
  signing up.
